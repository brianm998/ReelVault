# ReelVault Privacy Policy

*Last updated: June 2026*

ReelVault ("the app") is a video library management tool. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

## Data We Do Not Collect

The developer does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, crash reporters, or third-party SDKs that phone home.

## Photo Library

ReelVault requests access to your photo library to read and catalog videos stored on your device. This data never leaves your device except when you explicitly choose to upload a video to your own ReelVault server.

## Local Network

ReelVault uses your local Wi-Fi network to discover and connect to a ReelVault server you control. No connections are made to external servers operated by the developer.

## Your ReelVault Server

If you run a ReelVault server, all video files and catalog data are stored on infrastructure you own and control. The developer has no access to your server or its contents.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/brianm998/ReelVault/issues
