Your whole video library,

ReelVault is a fast, native catalog for big video collections. It doesn't edit — it organizes, finds, and shows off your footage, then hands clips to the editor of your choice. Think Lightroom, but for video.

⬇ Download ReelVault View on GitHub
Beta Desktop (macOS / Windows / Linux) and the Server are public releases. Mobile is beta only — iOS via TestFlight, Android via Google Play closed testing.
WHAT YOU CAN DO WITH IT
🎬
Find the right clip and drop it straight into your edit
Scroll thousands of clips with instant thumbnails. Filter by resolution, codec, camera, date, location, or your own tags — then drag the clip out of the grid right into Premiere, Resolve, or Final Cut to build your master. No importing, no launching, no fuss. (On phones and tablets it's the share sheet instead of drag.)
🏆
Keep track of your finished masters
Tag and collect delivered cuts in one “Masters” collection — or a smart collection that gathers them automatically (“final exports, this year, 4K+”). Search them in under a second, even across 100k items. No more hunting through Final_FINAL_v3.mov folders.
📱
See how a master looks on every screen
Run ReelVault Server on one machine and open the same master on your iPhone, iPad, Android phone, Android tablet, and any laptop on the Wi-Fi. Play the exact file on each device to check how your delivery reads in every presentation mode — before you ship it.
📸
Wrangle the family photos & videos
Point it at your home archive and let it catalog birthdays, trips, and holidays. Tag people and events, build collections per year, and find that one clip from the lake in 2019 right on the map view. Your library stays usable on every device — even offline.
✈️
Browse your clips away from the computer
Phones and tablets keep their own on-device catalog that works with no network at all. Curate, tag, and review footage on the couch or on a plane — then sync back to your desktop when you're home. Sync is two-way, and each catalog stays fully usable on its own.

A few more it's great at

  • Scout & select on set — review the day's takes on an iPad over Wi-Fi while the originals stay on the cards or NAS.
  • Cull duplicates & proxies — spot redundant copies and oversized files before they eat your storage.
  • Shared team vault — one always-on Server, everyone browses the same archive over the LAN (paired once, encrypted).
  • Stock & B-roll library — tag by mood, subject, and location so the right shot surfaces instantly next project.
