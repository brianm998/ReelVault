How will you use ReelVault?
Choose the setup that matches your workflow.

Just on this computer
ReelVault runs as a self-contained app. Your video library stays on your computer — no network configuration, no servers to maintain.
  • Works offline, always
  • Single-computer setup
  • No ports or firewall rules
  • Zero network configuration
📡
+ iPhone, iPad & Android over WiFi
Install a lightweight background service that streams your library to any iPhone, iPad, or Android device on the same WiFi. No cloud, no subscriptions — direct local network streaming.
  • Browse & play from iOS and Android devices
  • Auto-discovered on your WiFi
  • Upload footage from iPhone or Android
  • Pairs once with a 6-digit code
